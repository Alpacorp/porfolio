## The idea

Every December, Colombian families pray the **novena**, a Colombian Christmas prayer tradition held December 16–24. Many companies wanted to give that tradition to their customers and employees under their own brand, and have it work well on a phone.

## The product

- Navigation through **numbered steps** and a day picker for each day's reflection.
- Prayers, *gozos* (sung verses) and **Christmas carols**.
- **Mobile-first:** families use it together, phone in hand.
- **White label:** logo, background, illustrations, colors, title, favicon, social sharing image and custom blocks for each client, like the pizzeria's menu or Thanks To You's partner foundations.

## How it evolved

The first version started as a template in **Create React App** and was adapted for several companies. When I was working in the Marketing department at **Banco Caja Social**, we built the bank's own version, which I rewrote in **Vite + React 18** with semantic Spanish routes and touches like a snow effect. It went from a custom build to a **reusable product** that now carries the names of 8 companies.
