## The challenge

Opening a certificate of deposit (CDT) meant going to a branch. The bank wanted to move account opening and deposit collection to digital without giving up the security and compliance a financial product requires.

## What we built

I joined the team in my second year at the bank, after a year in the Marketing department. We built **the MVP and then the improved version**: the entire frontend for the opening flow and every integration with the services connected to the bank's core business.

- A **100% online** opening experience that's easy to follow and secure.
- Frontend in **React and Next.js**, middle-tier services in **NestJS**, and TypeScript across the stack.
- Code quality and security with **SonarCloud**, CI/CD on **Azure DevOps**, and user behavior tracking with **Google Analytics**.

## How we worked

We used **TDD (test-driven development)**, a methodology that was new to me and turned out to be the best I've worked with. At a bank, every release goes through strict quality and information security controls. That rigor is what let us ship a product that's still running exactly as we left it.

## The result

- We hit the account opening and deposit targets set by management **within the first six months**.
- We raised **over COP 2 billion (≈ USD 500K)** in the first two years.
- It was the **bank's best product three years in a row**, and the team won an award for it.
- It's still in production **exactly as we built it**.
