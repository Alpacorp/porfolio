## The business

Mr. Goma Tires sells used and new tires and offers auto services at **7 locations in Miami and Orlando**. It doesn't compete on catalog size or price, but on experience. The project's mission: **make buying a used tire online feel as clear as buying a new premium product**.

## What I built

I led and built the platform end to end, in English and for US shoppers.

- **Storefront:** tire-size search from the homepage, a catalog with faceted filters and counts, brand and size pages, and a product page with a gallery, zoom and a **3D tread view** showing depth in 1/32" and % of tread life left.
- **Checkout:** cart and checkout with **Stripe**, WhatsApp orders as an alternative, and a step-by-step quote tool connected to **n8n**.
- **Sales dashboard:** an inventory table with filters by location, plus an **AI chat (Claude, with tool use)**. Salespeople type what they're looking for in plain language, and the model applies the table filters.
- **SEO and analytics:** a Google Merchant Center feed, structured data, guides with FAQs, pages for each location and service, and consent-based analytics.

## Decisions that mattered

- **Spec-driven development** with Claude Code: over 35 documented features, each with its own spec, plan, tasks and measured results.
- **Saying no with data.** An SEO audit recommended consolidating "duplicate" URLs. The analysis showed those units had real price differences (a 22% median), so we kept them separate.
- **Performance on a budget:** a JavaScript size limit enforced in CI and hundreds of automated tests.
- **Security in production:** we detected and mitigated a bot attack of 150,000–200,000 fake visits a day.

## The result

- **CLS from 0.914 to 0** on mobile and from 0.525 to 0 on desktop, on the product page.
- **Mobile LCP from 4.5 s to 3.2 s** on the same page.
- The gallery zoom went from taking 1.7 s to being ready **before hover**, with images cut from 283 KB to 90 KB.
- **29% lower daily infrastructure cost** after mitigating the automated traffic.
