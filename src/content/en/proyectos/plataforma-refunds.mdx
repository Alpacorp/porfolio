## The context

At Mercado Libre and Mercado Pago, refunds are processed in batches across several countries and through different payment processors. Not all of them go through on their own: some need a person to review them, adjust them or resubmit them. The Refunds teams needed a new tool to manage that whole process.

## What I built

In my second year at OpsIT, I built the new platform, working closely with the backend team and the teams that own the APIs it integrates with.

- **Refund flows:** create them and customize each one's configuration.
- **Manual handling:** work through the refunds that can't be processed automatically.
- **Batches:** track every refund batch and act on its different execution outcomes.
- **Processors:** templates for each payment processor (Visa, Mastercard…).

## The result

A platform that was complex to build but is robust for the operations teams who manage refunds across countries. **Today it processes millions of refunds efficiently.**

After launch, I kept working in the same domain, now on the backend too, building Go verticals for Refunds.
