## The context

At an agency, development has to keep pace with campaigns: fixed dates, regulated brands and creative teams who expect to see their idea on screen exactly as they pictured it. I work as a **full stack developer for BBDO Mexico** and the agency's clients.

## What I've built

- **The agency's website:** the current corporate site (WordPress) and the **new Astro version**, with GSAP and View Transitions, now in testing.
- **Bayer:** the **Afrin and Tabcin sites, built from scratch in Drupal**.
- **Finamex:** *Adiós Inverfobia*, a campaign microsite with an **investor profile quiz** and lead capture connected to **HubSpot**.
- **San Rafael (Sigma Alimentos):** *Vida en Balance*, the site for its healthy product line with products, nutrition facts and recipes, plus **content management** for San Rafael Deli.
- **BMW Mexico:** **webmaster**, managing content and building the HTML for **every promotional email**.
- **FedEx Mexico:** design proposals for a campaign landing page and HTML emails.

## What it shows

Regulated brands (pharma and finance), consumer goods and global corporations. A different stack each time, from Drupal and WordPress to React with Vite and Astro, always with the marketing integrations a campaign demands.
